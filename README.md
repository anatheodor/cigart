# The Cigar(t) — Chained Diegetic Displays (WebXR)

A browser-based WebXR remediation of a two-screen 2D animation, part of the paper
*Bridging 2D Animation and Virtual Reality: Chained Diegetic Displays, Gaze-Driven
Montage, and Discoverability*.

**Authors:** Stefania Symeonidou & Anastassios Theodoropoulos
— Department of Performing and Digital Arts, University of the Peloponnese.

## Run it
Live: https://anatheodor.github.io/cigart/
- On desktop: open the link, click **Start**, drag to look around.
- On Meta Quest: open the link in the **Browser**, click **Start**, then **Enter VR**.

## Controls
- **Drag / look** — passive; only tracks attention and decides the ending.
- **A / B** (or controller **trigger**) — step inside a screen. **Esc** — step back.
- **F** — gaze-pacing game-mode. **S** — export session data (JSON).

## Notes
Two synchronised screens play one story across the space. Your gaze writes your own
montage and, at the close, chooses the ending (bloom vs. wither). Built with A-Frame.
